## 1. Context: Who Is Jellysmack and What Makes It Unique
Jellysmack is a global leader in the creator economy, specializing in **amplification**, **multi-platform distribution**, and **monetization** for top-tier creators. Unlike traditional OTT platforms (Zype, Vimeo OTT, Brightcove) or white-label solutions (Vodlix, VPlayed), Jellysmack operates as a **next-generation MCN + content studio + proprietary tech layer** — helping creators scale views, revenue, and presence across platforms.

### Key Company Facts
- Founded: 2016, headquartered in Paris (France), offices in New York, Los Angeles, and others
- Company type: private, former unicorn (peak valuation ~$3B in 2023), current estimated valuation ~$1–1.5B after 2024–2025 restructuring
- Investors: SoftBank, Highland Europe, Partech, creator investors
- Scale: 500+ partnered creators, billions of monthly views, 125M+ unique users in the US (cross-platform)
- Revenue model: revenue share on incremental ad revenue + upfront licensing of back catalog + brand partnerships
- Key milestones: partnerships with MrBeast, PewDiePie, Bailey Sarian and hundreds more; launch of Jellysmack Originals (owned channels); 2024–2025 restructuring with increased focus on IP and originals

### What Makes Jellysmack Unique in This Competitive Landscape
- Not a SaaS platform — managed service + proprietary AI/tech for selected top creators
- Selective onboarding: only high-potential creators accepted
- True multi-platform amplification: YouTube → TikTok → Snapchat → Facebook → Instagram → Twitch and more
- Upfront cash for back catalog licensing (Jellyboard / Catalog Licensing)
- Jellysmack Originals — owned channels in beauty, gaming, sports, movies, etc.
- Brand & media licensing: direct deals with brands and media companies

## 11. Strategic Conclusions

**Where Jellysmack Can Be Attacked**
- Accessibility: selective model — competitors with self-serve (Uscreen, Vimeo) win mid-tier creators
- Control: revenue share repels creators wanting 100% income
- Independence: platform dependency creates risk
- Brand awareness: less known outside top-creator circle

**Where Jellysmack Is Hard to Beat**
- Scale and top creators: MrBeast-level partnerships — huge social capital
- Multi-platform amplification: hard to replicate without their tech/connections
- Cash advances: help creators invest in growth
- AI + data: proprietary tools for optimization

**Key Strategic Insight**  
Jellysmack is a **"creator accelerator"** for the top tier — not a universal OTT platform. They win through scale, technology, and access to big names, but it’s a closed club. For mid-tier creators/businesses they are not a competitor to Vimeo OTT, Uscreen or Zype — those offer self-serve and control. Jellysmack dominates where **managed growth** and **multi-platform scale** are needed, but the risk lies in platform dependency and selective approach.
