## 8. Weaknesses & Vulnerabilities

- No API (per GetApp data) — serious limitation for devs/enterprise integrations
- No free trial — prospect invests time in demo without self-test
- Low public reviews: 5–20 vs 500+ for Brightcove — harder trust for cold leads
- Weak international brand: outside APAC virtually unknown
- No full FAST/Linear Playout — broadcasters go to Zype
- Support via ticket/email only: no phone in US/EU time zones
- "Indian vendor" perception: US/EU procurement may require local entity
- Upfront payment: psychological barrier for OPEX-accustomed companies
