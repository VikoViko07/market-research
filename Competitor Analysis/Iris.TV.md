**AI Content Data Marketplace for CTV & Streaming Advertising**
*March 2026 · Sources: iris.tv, Viant Technology (NASDAQ: DSP) filings, AdExchanger, Marketing Dive, BusinessWire, LeadIQ, CBInsights, Innovid, Seedtag press releases*

---

> **⚠️ Critical Context Before Reading**
> IRIS.TV is not a video creation, video enhancement, or video player platform. It is a **content data infrastructure layer** for CTV advertising — invisible to end viewers, embedded in the programmatic ecosystem. Comparing it to Synthesia, HeyGen, or Minute.ly is a category error. The correct frame: IRIS.TV is to CTV advertising what a taxonomy standard is to a library — it structures, identifies, and activates content-level signals so that third parties (advertisers, data companies, DSPs) can use them. This distinction defines every section of this report.
>
> **Second critical context:** IRIS.TV was **acquired by Viant Technology (NASDAQ: DSP) in November 2024**. It is no longer an independent company. This fundamentally changes its competitive dynamics, vendor stability profile, and strategic trajectory.

**Note on messaging shift:** The word "only" has been replaced by "leading" since the Viant acquisition. This is likely a legal/compliance adjustment — "only" is a bold exclusive claim. "Leading" is softer and more defensible.

### One-Line Value Proposition
> *"Our content identifier, the IRIS_ID, enables our partners to build scalable advertising solutions for contextual and brand-suitability planning, targeting, and measurement."*
> — iris.tv

### What IRIS.TV Actually Does
IRIS.TV operates as **infrastructure**, not a product end-users interact with. It:

1. **Ingests** video content from publishers, broadcasters, and CMS platforms (1,400+ integrations, 75M+ videos indexed)
2. **Analyzes** each video frame-by-frame using AI to extract contextual, emotional, and brand-suitability signals
3. **Assigns** a universal content identifier — the IRIS_ID — to each video
4. **Passes** the IRIS_ID through the programmatic bidstream, enabling buyers to target by content type without user-level data

**Who their actual clients are:**
- **Publishers / content owners:** LG, Vizio, Univision, Xumo, Tubi (Fox), Blue Ant Media — use IRIS_ID to monetize content contextually
- **Data partners:** GumGum, Pixability, 4D, Captify, Silverpush, KERV.ai, Mobian, Sightly, SpotRunner — enrich IRIS_ID with their contextual segments
- **Advertisers / agencies:** Carl's Jr./PMG, USIM, outdoor lifestyle brands — use IRIS-enabled segments for CTV targeting
- **Ad platforms:** Viant DSP, Magnite Clearline, Innovid — execute against IRIS_ID in the bidstream

**They do NOT serve:** video creators, content producers, L&D teams, marketing creators, or anyone who makes videos. Any pitch into those categories is a complete category miss.

## 3. Product Portfolio

### 3.1 Core Product Stack (Verified March 2026)

| Product | What It Does | Who Uses It |
|---------|-------------|-------------|
| **IRIS_ID** | Universal content identifier — assigns a persistent ID to each video for cross-platform recognition | Publishers, DSPs, data partners, measurement firms |
| **IRIS Content Data Platform** | Ingests, normalizes, and AI-analyzes video metadata at scale (frame-by-frame) | Publishers integrating their content libraries |
| **IRIS-enabled™ Ecosystem** | Marketplace connecting publishers, data partners, and ad buyers through the IRIS_ID standard | Advertisers, agencies, data companies |
| **IRIS Context Macros** | Passes content signals into the programmatic bidstream for pre-bid targeting | DSPs and SSPs executing contextual buys |
| **IRIS-enabled Content Report** | Real-time campaign analytics tied to specific content environments (KPIs by context/emotion category) | Advertisers optimizing live campaigns |
| **Pre-Bid Targeting** | Allows buyers to select content categories before auction, not post-buy | Media buyers and planners |

### 3.2 The IRIS_ID — Core Technical Asset

The IRIS_ID is the company's most defensible asset and its primary moat. Key properties:
- **Universal:** Works across 1,400+ platforms, CMSs, streaming apps, and smart TV manufacturers
- **Persistent:** The same ID follows a video across distribution platforms — a movie on Tubi and Xumo has the same IRIS_ID
- **AI-enriched:** Each ID carries contextual signals (genre, emotion, topic, brand suitability) generated by AI frame-level analysis
- **Privacy-safe:** No PII attached — targeting is content-based, not user-based
- **Open:** Viant committed the IRIS_ID will remain available to all DSPs and partners, not exclusive to Viant

### 3.3 Post-Acquisition Integration (Viant, July 2025)

Viant launched IRIS-enabled Content Report and Pre-Bid Targeting natively inside its DSP in July 2025. This is the first deep post-acquisition product integration. It means:
- Viant DSP users get IRIS data natively — no separate contract or integration needed
- IRIS.TV gains distribution scale through Viant's buyer base
- Competitors using Viant DSP also benefit — the data remains neutral (confirmed by Viant CMO)

> ⚠️ **Tension point:** Viant's native integration gives Viant DSP users a friction-free IRIS experience that competing DSPs cannot match. Even if the data is technically available to all, the UX advantage accrues to Viant. This is the core conflict of the post-acquisition story.

## 5. Pricing & Revenue Model

### 5.1 Revenue Model
**No public pricing.** IRIS.TV does not operate on a subscription or self-serve model. Revenue flows through:

- **Data licensing fees:** Data partners (GumGum, Pixability, 4D, etc.) pay to access and enrich IRIS_ID content signals
- **Publisher integration fees:** Content owners may pay for IRIS_ID integration services
- **Platform access fees:** DSPs and SSPs integrating IRIS Context Macros into their bidstream
- **Post-acquisition:** IRIS.TV revenue is folded into Viant's reporting — no standalone financials disclosed

## 8. Competitive Landscape

> **Methodology note:** IRIS.TV competes in the **CTV content data and contextual targeting** segment — not video creation, video players, or recommendation engines. The correct competitive set is ad tech companies that provide contextual targeting signals for CTV/streaming environments.

| Competitor | What They Do | Core Strength vs IRIS.TV | Threat Level |
|------------|-------------|--------------------------|--------------|
| **ZEFR** | Video-level brand safety and contextual targeting for YouTube and CTV | Strong YouTube coverage; established brand safety focus; independent (not tied to one DSP) | 🔴 HIGH |
| **DoubleVerify (DV)** | Brand safety, fraud detection, contextual targeting across digital and CTV | Public company ($600M+ revenue), massive scale, integrated with all major DSPs without ownership conflict | 🔴 HIGH |
| **Integral Ad Science (IAS)** | Brand safety, viewability, contextual segments for CTV and digital | Public company, deep DSP integrations, no DSP ownership conflict | 🔴 HIGH |
| **GumGum / Verity** | In-video contextual analysis, attention intelligence | Direct IRIS partner — also a partial competitor for contextual segment creation | 🟡 MEDIUM |
| **Pixability** | YouTube and CTV contextual targeting, audience segments | Strong YouTube + CTV coverage; also an IRIS data partner — hybrid partner/competitor | 🟡 MEDIUM |
| **Wurl** | FAST channel distribution + contextual data (scene-level) | Native CTV data from FAST channel infrastructure; Viant already integrated Wurl separately | 🟡 MEDIUM |
| **Oracle Advertising (deprecated)** | Contextual segments, data marketplace | Oracle shut down its advertising business (2023) — vacates a segment IRIS.TV can capture | ⬜ EXITED |
| **Streaming platforms themselves** | Netflix, Disney+, Amazon direct-sold contextual data | Walled garden data — not available in open programmatic; but direct-sold budgets divert from IRIS-enabled buys | ⚫ STRUCTURAL |

**Most dangerous competitive dynamic:** DoubleVerify and IAS are both publicly traded, DSP-neutral, and already integrated across the ecosystem. They have stronger balance sheets, broader reach, and no Viant ownership conflict. If they accelerate CTV contextual offerings (both have announced CTV expansion), they can displace IRIS_ID as the de facto standard.

## 9. Win / Loss Summary

### IRIS.TV Likely Wins When…
- Advertiser needs **video-level** (not page-level) contextual targeting for CTV — IRIS_ID is the only cross-platform standard at this granularity
- Privacy-first targeting is required — no PII, no cookies, fully content-based
- Publisher needs to monetize content contextually without exposing editorial data directly to buyers
- Campaign uses Viant DSP — native integration gives friction-free access with real-time reporting
- Multi-DSP, multi-platform campaign requires a common content identifier — IRIS_ID is the only cross-ecosystem standard
- Brand suitability is a requirement alongside contextual targeting — IRIS_ID carries both signals

### IRIS.TV Likely Loses When…
- Campaign targets inventory on Netflix, Disney+, Amazon, or other walled gardens — IRIS_ID not present
- Buyer has existing DoubleVerify or IAS contract and wants unified brand safety + contextual from one vendor
- DSP relationship favors a competitor (e.g., buyer primarily uses The Trade Desk, which has its own content targeting solutions)
- Buyer requires standalone, auditable financial transparency — not available post-acquisition
- Competing DSP reduces IRIS.TV integration due to Viant ownership perception — creates coverage gaps

### Verified Risk Signals
- Acquisition by a DSP creates real or perceived neutrality risk — competing DSPs may deprioritize IRIS integration over time
- Walled garden gap (Netflix, Disney+, Amazon) limits reach on the fastest-growing premium CTV inventory
- No standalone public financials post-acquisition — opacity on growth trajectory
- 37-person team for a 75M+ video index is a resource constraint on expansion pace

## 10. Regulatory & Strategic Context

### Privacy Regulation as Tailwind
IRIS.TV is structurally advantaged by the direction of privacy regulation:
- **Cookie deprecation** (ongoing) removes user-level targeting — content-level targeting fills the gap
- **GDPR / CCPA compliance** is built into the IRIS_ID model — no PII collected or transmitted
- **EU AI Act** (August 2026) — IRIS.TV analyzes existing content (not generating synthetic media), likely outside the strictest requirements. No compliance gap identified.

**Positioning implication:** Every tightening of privacy regulation is a tailwind for IRIS_ID adoption. IRIS.TV's messaging around "privacy-first" is not defensive — it is genuinely strategic.

### CTV Market Growth
CTV advertising is IRIS.TV's direct growth lever:
- Viant reported CTV revenue up ~50% YoY in Q3 2024
- Netflix, Disney+, Amazon have all launched ad-supported tiers — dramatically expanding addressable CTV inventory
- Brands are shifting linear TV budgets to CTV — driving demand for CTV-specific targeting signals

> ⚠️ **Irony:** The largest beneficiaries of CTV growth (Netflix, Disney+, Amazon) operate as walled gardens and do not participate in the IRIS_ID ecosystem. IRIS.TV benefits from the CTV macro trend but cannot reach the fastest-growing premium inventory within it.

### Post-Acquisition Strategic Scenarios

| Scenario | Description | Probability | Implication |
|----------|-------------|-------------|-------------|
| **Full integration into Viant** | IRIS_ID becomes a Viant-exclusive feature over 2–3 years | Low-Medium | Ecosystem trust collapses; other DSPs defect |
| **Independent marketplace maintained** | Viant keeps IRIS_ID open to all DSPs as a neutral standard | Medium-High | Current trajectory; Viant benefits from ecosystem data richness |
| **IRIS_ID becomes industry standard** | Adopted as the de facto CTV content ID across all DSPs and SSPs | Medium | Best case for Viant — creates data moat that grows with ecosystem |
| **DoubleVerify / IAS displaces IRIS_ID** | Public competitors accelerate CTV contextual and capture the standard | Medium | IRIS.TV's window for standardization closes within 2–3 years |
