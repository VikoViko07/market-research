## 1. Context – The Publisher and Its Place in the Series

The Globe and Mail is Canada's national newspaper, founded in 1844 in Toronto. Unlike Reuters (B2B), it is a **B2C publisher** that sells journalism directly to readers. It targets an elite Canadian audience focused on national politics, business, finance, and culture.

**Key unique feature:** The Globe and Mail is the only publisher in the series that built its own AI platform (**Sophi**), spun it out as a separate product, sold it to a third party, and remains its largest client. It turned from a publisher into an AI vendor and then decided to focus back on core journalism.

## 2. History – From Newspaper to AI Platform

- **1844**: Founded in Toronto.
- **2003**: One of the first quality newspapers to introduce a digital paywall.
- **2012**: Introduced metered paywall, inspired by The New York Times.
- **2012–2014**: Hired data specialists from BlackBerry.
- **2015**: Launched internal AI platform **Sophi**.
- **2020**: Sophi automated 99% of digital content.
- **2021**: Spun off into Sophi Inc.
- **2022**: Sophi offered as a commercial product to other publishers.
- **2023**: Sold to Mediahuis (via Mather Economics). Globe remains the main client.
- **2024**: Won Cannes Lions “Publisher of the Year” (Gold).
- **November 2024**: Joined lawsuit against OpenAI with other Canadian media.
- **2025–2026**: “Super app” strategy – narrative articles, games, conversational AI.

**Editorial Note:** Sophi is a unique case studied academically as a model of AI integration in journalism.

## 3. Business Model and Finance

- Estimated revenue: ~$299 million (2025).
- **>70%** of revenue comes from subscriptions — one of the highest rates in the industry.
- Main revenue streams: Digital + print subscriptions, advertising, Globe Events, Globe Content Studio (B2B branded content).

## 4. Sophi – The Flagship AI Project and Its Fate

**Sophi** is the most documented AI case in the series.

### What Sophi Does (Core Components)
- **Sophi Score**: Scores every article for its value to subscriptions, advertising, retention, etc.
- **Sophi Site Automation**: Automatically curates the homepage, newsletters, and social media every 10 minutes (99% of content).
- **Sophi Dynamic Paywall**: Fully personalized, real-time paywall that decides for each user and each article whether to show a paywall, ask for email, or allow free access.
- **Sophi Content Paywall**: Predicts article value before publication.
- **Facebook Automation**: Decides what to post on Facebook.
- **Snowplow Integration**: Uses behavioral data for all ML models.

**Results:**
- +222% registrations
- +51% subscription conversions
- -41% cost of acquiring subscribers via Facebook

### History
- Built with ex-BlackBerry specialists.
- Introduced gradually to gain editorial trust (recommendations → automation of low-impact content → full autonomy).
- 2023: Sold to Mather Economics (Mediahuis subsidiary). Now used by 49+ sites in 4 countries.

## 5. Current AI Tools in the Newsroom

### Production & Newsroom Tools
- Sophi Dynamic Paywall and Site Automation (still in use)
- AI transcription of interviews
- Topic extraction
- Automated curation and recommendations
- Reader insights and churn prediction (reduced churn by 140%)
- AI-generated article summaries (experimental)
- Conversational discovery tools (experimental)
- Narrated articles (AI voice) – in the mobile app
- Personalized homepage

### In Globe Content Studio (B2B)
- **Midjourney** – for conceptual visuals and illustrations
- **Runway** – to turn images into short animated videos
- Strict policy: AI is a creative accelerator, not a replacement; no training on Globe content.

### Editorial Guidelines (Version 2.0, 2025)
- AI only with human oversight — no auto-publishing.
- Generative AI **not suitable** for core writing or editing.
- Mandatory labeling when AI is used.
- Risks (hallucinations, bias) explicitly addressed.

## 6. Reader-Facing AI – “Super App” Vision

The Globe aims to evolve from a news site into a **multimodal daily companion**:

- **Narrated Articles** (AI voice) – production
- Digital games – growing
- AI summaries – testing
- Conversational discovery – early prototype
- Personalized homepage and newsletters – live

## 7. AI Licensing and Legal Position – The Paradox

- **November 2024**: Joined lawsuit against OpenAI in Ontario Superior Court (with CBC, Toronto Star, Postmedia, etc.).
  - Accusations: Copyright infringement, bypassing paywalls, unjust enrichment.
  - Claim: Up to C$20,000 per article + punitive damages.
- **No public licensing deals** with AI companies (unlike Reuters, Le Monde, The Guardian, etc.).
- Uses GenAI tools (Midjourney, Runway) while suing OpenAI for training on their content.

**Editorial Note:** This is a “sue first, license later” strategy or a firm stance on copyright.

## 8. Strengths and Weaknesses (AI Focus)

**Strengths:**
- 10+ years of data culture and ML maturity (Sophi legacy).
- High subscription revenue share thanks to AI.
- Strong audience-first culture.
- Proven churn reduction via AI.

**Weaknesses:**
- Sold Sophi → lost exclusive competitive advantage.
- No licensing revenue from archive yet.
- Cautious approach to GenAI in core journalism.
- Small Canadian market limits scale.

## 9. Overall Assessment

- **ML / Traditional AI Maturity:** Leader in the series.
- **GenAI Maturity:** Cautious / experimental.
- **Subscription Model:** Very strong.
- **Reader-Facing AI:** In development (voice, games, conversational).
- **AI Licensing:** Open question (lawsuit ongoing).
- **Competitive Position in Canada:** Dominant.

**Summary in one sentence:**  
The Globe and Mail built one of the most advanced AI systems for subscription optimization (Sophi), sold it, continues to use it, applies GenAI cautiously outside core writing, and is currently suing OpenAI while pursuing a “super app” strategy to become a daily companion for Canadian readers.
