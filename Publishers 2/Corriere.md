## 2. AI Strategy – Simple Overview

Corriere uses AI in a very **practical and business-focused** way. They do not talk much about it publicly (unlike Spiegel’s Ole Reissmann), but the results are strong, especially in growing subscriptions.

### 2.1 Partnership with OpenAI (2024)

In July 2024, RCS signed a major global partnership with OpenAI – the first such deal among major Italian publishers.

**What they built:**
- An AI assistant inside the **L’Economia** (Business) section.
- You can ask questions like “What will happen to Italian banks in 2026?” and the assistant answers using thousands of Corriere articles.

**Strengths:**
- Fast launch and real product.
- Helps readers stay longer on the site and increases subscription conversions (+20–30% in this section).
- Gives Corriere a modern image in Italy.

**Weaknesses:**
- Many journalists were unhappy – they felt the newspaper was “selling” its content to OpenAI.
- Too much dependence on one company (OpenAI).
- Not very transparent about how old articles are used for training AI.

**What would be useful to implement:**
- Create a full “Ask Corriere” AI assistant for the entire newspaper and 148-year archive (not only business).
- Add another AI partner (e.g. Anthropic or European Mistral) to reduce dependence on OpenAI.

### 2.2 Smart Paywall (Dynamic Paywall)

The AI analyses how you read and decides when to show the subscription offer.

**Strengths:**
- Extremely successful. Corriere reached ~727,000 digital subscribers – one of the best results among quality newspapers in Europe.
- Directly increases revenue.

**Weaknesses:**
- The model is not shown publicly (black box).
- Sometimes it fails to convince readers who leave the site quickly.

**What would be useful to implement:**
- Add a “churn prediction” model – predict who might cancel and offer them a discount or bonus in time.
- Connect the paywall with the AI assistant so the chatbot can offer a subscription at the right moment.

### 2.3 Text-to-Speech (Article Audio)

Since 2023, any article can be listened to with two custom “Corriere voices” that sound professional.

**Strengths:**
- Very convenient – people listen while driving, walking, or cooking.
- The voices are branded and sound high-quality.

**Weaknesses:**
- It only reads the text. There is no music, pauses, or real podcast feeling.

**What would be useful to implement:**
- Turn articles into real **AI podcasts** automatically (with music and editing), similar to the experiment at Der Spiegel.

### 2.4 Video Platform & Player

Corriere uses **JW Player** (same as Der Spiegel). They also run **Corriere TV** and launched FAST channels (24/7 free video streams on Samsung TV, Amazon, etc.).

**Strengths:**
- Simple and reliable technology.
- Old videos generate advertising revenue with almost no extra cost.

**Weaknesses:**
- No tool to automatically turn long articles into short videos.

**What would be useful to implement (Biggest quick win):**
- Adopt **Videofy** (the free open-source tool released by VG).  
  One long article → 30–60 second video in one click.  
  These videos can be used on YouTube, TikTok, and FAST channels. This would bring more views and more ad revenue at almost zero cost.

### 2.5 What Corriere Is Missing (Main Gaps)

- No general reader-facing AI assistant (only in the business section).
- No internal reports like **Gender Report** (Spiegel) to check balance in their own journalism.
- They do not sell or license their huge 148-year archive to other AI companies or universities.

**Quick wins for 2026:**
1. Launch full “Ask Corriere” AI assistant.
2. Implement Videofy for automatic video creation.
3. Create automatic AI podcasts.
4. Build a simple diversity/quality report.

**Bottom line – Simple summary:**

Corriere della Sera uses AI **very practically and successfully** to make money, especially through subscriptions. Their approach is business-oriented and effective.

However, they are quite **closed and cautious** in communication. They experiment less publicly than Der Spiegel and give readers very few AI tools to play with.

If in 2026 they add:
- **Videofy** (automatic videos),
- A full “Ask Corriere” assistant,
- Real AI podcasts,

they will significantly improve both reader engagement and revenue.
